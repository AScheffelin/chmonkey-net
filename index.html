About Me

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at Columbia University, where I also earned my MA in 2023 and MS in 2026[cite: 6]. My research interests lie between commutative algebra and algebraic geometry, with a particular focus on local cohomology[cite: 1]. Specifically, I study mixed characteristic problems, perfectoid techniques, homotopical methods, and prismatic cohomology[cite: 6].

Prior to joining Columbia University, I completed my B.S. in Mathematics (cum laude) at the University of Washington[cite: 6]. During 2026, I am also a Visiting Student at the University of Tokyo working with Shunsuke Takagi and Kazuma Shimomoto[cite: 6].

Preprints & Research

The Second Vanishing Theorem in Ramified Mixed Characteristic[cite: 6]
Alex Scheffelin
Preprint, 2026.

I prove the last remaining case of the Second Vanishing Theorem for local cohomology in ramified mixed characteristic[cite: 6]. This work extends naturally into investigating bounds on cohomological dimension and the relationship between local cohomology modules and the cohomology of formal schemes[cite: 1, 4].
