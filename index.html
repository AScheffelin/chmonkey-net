About Me

I am a 5th year PhD student in Mathematics at Columbia University, studying under Johan de Jong. I am on the job market this year. My research interests lie between commutative algebra and algebraic geometry, with a particular focus on local cohomology. More specifically, I like to use geometric methods to investigate algebraic problems. At the moment this has to do with local cohomology of regular local rings, with a focus on mixed characteristic. I have begun thinking about other mixed characteristic topics as well.

Prior to attending Columbia University, I completed my B.S. in Mathematics at the University of Washington. During the summer of 2026, I was a visiting student at the University of Tokyo working with Shunsuke Takagi and Kazuma Shimomoto.
